# Writing project

Ask which approved samples belong to this language, genre and audience. Draft from the supplied facts. In procedures use one action per step and consistent terminology. In manuscripts preserve denominators, uncertainty, direction and association versus causation. Review edits against the original in a diff.

Choose critique, edit or draft according to the request. Preserve author intent and distinctive phrasing. Distinguish missing evidence from a wording problem. Protect numbers, negation, conditions and citations. For substantial manuscripts, establish the argument and evidence before expanding prose.
