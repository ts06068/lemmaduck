# Abductive research

Record the observation that needs explaining and the assumptions already supplied by the researcher. Develop competing mechanisms before favoring one. Include a mundane explanation, a measurement or selection artifact where relevant, and a hypothesis that would challenge the initial framing.

For each hypothesis, state its evidence, missing evidence, discriminating prediction and a feasible test or counterexample. Distinguish observation, proposed mechanism and verified result. Report when the available evidence cannot distinguish explanations. Preserve rejected hypotheses and the evidence that changed the decision. Ask the researcher to resolve scientific choices that remain underdetermined.
